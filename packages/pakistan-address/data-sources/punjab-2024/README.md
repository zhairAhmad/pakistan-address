# Punjab: Board of Revenue notification of 18 December 2024

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Document | Government of the Punjab, Revenue Department, Notification No. 479-2024/6904-DIR(DEV&G), Lahore, 18 December 2024 |
| What it says | Divides the province into **10 divisions, 41 districts and 156 tehsils**, "in supersession of all previous Notifications", with immediate effect (Sections 5 and 6, Punjab Land Revenue Act, 1967) |
| Signed by | Nabeel Javed, PAS, Senior Member, Board of Revenue, Punjab; copy forwarded for publication in the official Gazette |
| Copy used | A 4-page scan published as a PDF by Minute Mirror: <https://minutemirror.com.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Notification-of-Divisions-Districts-Tehsils-in-the-Punjab.pdf> (855 KB). The original on a government site was not found |
| Retrieved | 7 October 2026 |

`punjab-notification-2024-12-18.json` is a **hand transcription** of the schedule, in the notification's spelling (one capitalisation was normalised: "Isa khel" is written "Isa Khel"). It was checked against the stated totals: 10 divisions, 41 districts and 156 tehsils, and the apply script refuses to run if they do not add up. The scan itself is not stored in the repository.

`../../scripts/apply-punjab-2024.mjs` merged it into `data/pakistan.json`; its changes are listed in `apply-report.md`.

Reported by: [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1880708/notification-confirms-gujrat-as-10th-division-of-punjab) and [Minute Mirror](https://minutemirror.com.pk/new-notification-of-divisions-districtstehsils-in-punjab-324751/).
